About HomeStride

Designed to make home-service coordination clearer.

HomeStride is a fictional full-stack platform that demonstrates how customers, administrators and technicians can manage household service requests together.

The concept

One place to organize everyday household support.

Finding help is only one part of a home-service experience. Customers also need clear appointments, visible assignments and reliable progress information.

HomeStride brings those stages into one connected workflow. Customers submit requests, administrators coordinate suitable technicians, and technicians record progress through completion.

Platform principles

Built around clarity, accountability and practical use.

The platform’s features support a straightforward service experience for every role.

01

Clear Information

Service details, starting prices, appointment preferences and request statuses remain easy to find.

02

Defined Ownership

Each request moves from customer submission to administrative review and technician responsibility.

03

Visible Progress

Status history and technician notes help customers understand what has happened at every stage.

04

Role-Based Access

Customers, administrators and technicians receive separate dashboards with appropriate information and actions.

Behind the platform

A complete portfolio demonstration—not only a landing page.

HomeStride combines a responsive public website with secure authentication, REST API endpoints, role permissions and persistent PostgreSQL data.
