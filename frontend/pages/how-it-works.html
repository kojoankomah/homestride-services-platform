One connected service workflow

From household problem to completed service.

HomeStride connects customers, administrators and technicians through a clear request and progress-tracking process.

The service journey

Four stages keep every request organized.

Each role receives the information and actions needed at the appropriate stage of the service.

  1. 01

    Customer

    Submit the Request

    Select a service, choose or add an address, provide a preferred appointment time and describe the work required.

    Pending
  2. 02

    Administrator

    Review and Assign

    Review the request details and assign an available technician whose skills match the selected service.

    Assigned
  3. 03

    Technician

    Begin the Work

    View the assigned job, begin the service and provide a note that becomes part of the request’s status history.

    In Progress
  4. 04

    Technician and Customer

    Complete and Track

    Mark the work as completed while the customer retains a visible record of the request and its progress timeline.

    Completed

Role-specific experiences

One platform with three focused dashboards.

Each dashboard presents the information and controls relevant to its user.

01

Customer Dashboard

Create service requests, manage addresses, review assignments and follow progress history.

  • Request household services
  • Track current statuses
  • Review completed work
02

Administrator Dashboard

Review incoming requests, search records, monitor totals and assign suitable technicians.

  • Manage service requests
  • View technician skills
  • Coordinate assignments
03

Technician Dashboard

View assigned jobs and record the transition from assigned work to active and completed service.

  • Review job information
  • Add progress notes
  • Complete assigned work

Status guide

Know exactly what each request status means.

Consistent status labels make the next stage of every service request easier to understand.

Pending
The request has been submitted and awaits review.
Assigned
A technician has been selected for the service.
In Progress
The assigned technician has started the work.
Completed
The service has been marked as finished.
